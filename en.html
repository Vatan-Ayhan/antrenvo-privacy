ATRENVO — Privacy policy

App and contact

ATRENVO – Gym Tracker | Publisher: antrenvo | Contact: ayhanozyazici@gmail.com | Last updated: 20 September 2026

Information on your device

Your programs, workout and set logs, durations, RIR values, body measurements and optional pain, sleep and fatigue reports are stored in the app database on your device. They are used for workout tracking and personalized suggestions. There is no developer-operated account system or cloud synchronization. Smart Coach uses local exercise information and rules; conversations are not sent to an external answer service.

Advertising and consent services

Google AdMob provides ads for adults; Google UMP manages consent. Google advertising services may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device/account identifiers, interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Your choices

Workout, measurement, health, pain and chat content is not added to ad requests. The app requests non-personalized ads, which can still involve technical data processing. UMP offers consent choices where required. You can change available ad permissions under “Privacy and ad preferences” in the app. You can manage your advertising identifier in Android settings.

Age range

ATRENVO is intended for ages 13 and above. Your selected age range is stored on your device instead of a full date of birth. Ads are not loaded when you select ages 13–17 or prefer not to specify. You can reset your age preference in the privacy section.

Backups and external links

Android backup and device transfer may copy app data depending on your device and account settings; this is not a developer-operated backup server. YouTube links are subject to that service’s privacy terms. This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages; visits to this website are subject to GitHub’s privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until deleted. You can delete workout history and body measurements on their respective screens. Clearing app data in Android settings permanently removes all local records. Manage operating-system backups through your Google/Android account settings. Google’s policy governs retention and deletion of advertising data processed by Google. Contact ayhanozyazici@gmail.com for privacy requests. The developer cannot remotely access your local records.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and its update date will be revised.
